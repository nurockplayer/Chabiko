## Goal

Define the primary Japanese learner personas and jobs-to-be-done that guide Chabiko's v1 curriculum, onboarding, content model, and practice design.

## Scope

- Define 4–6 Japanese learner personas, such as Taiwan travel learner, university/credit learner, business or service-industry learner, study-abroad or exam-oriented learner, and Chinese media/culture interest learner.
- For each persona, document: motivation, learning context, desired outcome, pain points, likely session length, and strongest retention hook.
- Rank personas for Chabiko v1.
- Map each top persona to existing or planned Chabiko surfaces.

## Out of scope

- Building onboarding UI.
- Writing full curriculum content.
- Running user interviews.
- Adding analytics implementation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A short product note exists under `docs/product/` or another agreed planning location.
- [ ] Personas are prioritized for v1, not just listed.
- [ ] Each persona has a clear job-to-be-done statement.
- [ ] Each top persona maps to at least one v1 learning path or product surface.
